About UD'S Academy

Learn. Build. Lead. Since 2010.

Our vision

To be a college where students learn by building real things, and graduate ready to lead teams in technology.

Our mission

Teach strong fundamentals, put every student on real projects from year one, and keep close ties with industry.

Core values

Integrity

We do honest work and own our results.

Curiosity

We ask why before we ask how.

Craft

We build things carefully and finish them.

Service

We use what we learn to help others.

Our history

  1. 2010

    UD'S Academy opens with 120 students and two departments.

  2. 2014

    The new campus library and computer labs open.

  3. 2018

    The Artificial Intelligence and Data Science department is founded.

  4. 2021

    First industry-sponsored innovation lab starts.

  5. 2025

    More than 2,000 students and 5,000 alumni.
