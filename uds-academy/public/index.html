About UD'S Academy

Founded in 2010, UD'S Academy is an engineering college built around one idea: students learn best by making things. Every semester includes a project, every department has working labs, and industry partners help shape our courses.

Our programs in Computer Science, Electronics and Communication, and Artificial Intelligence and Data Science prepare graduates for careers in software, hardware, and data.
